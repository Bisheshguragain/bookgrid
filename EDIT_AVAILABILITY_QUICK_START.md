# Quick Start: Editing Availability Rules

## How to Edit an Availability Rule

### Step 1: Find the Rule
- Navigate to **Availability** page (`/app/availability`)
- Locate the day and time slot you want to edit in the weekly calendar

### Step 2: Click Edit
- Click the **✏️ Edit** button on the rule card
- The form will open with current values pre-filled

### Step 3: Make Changes
- Modify any field:
  - **Day of Week**: Change which day the rule applies to
  - **Start/End Time**: Adjust your available hours
  - **Buffer Times**: Update buffer before/after appointments

### Step 4: Save
- Click **💾 Update Rule** to save your changes
- Or click **Cancel** to discard changes

## Tips

✅ **Quick Edit**: Need to adjust just one thing? Edit is faster than delete + recreate!

✅ **Validate First**: The form will show errors if times are invalid - fix them before saving

✅ **Mobile Friendly**: Edit works perfectly on mobile devices too

✅ **Safe Changes**: Your original rule stays until you click Update

## Example Use Cases

### Scenario 1: Extend Your Hours
**Before:** Monday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM  
**Edit:** Change end time to 6:00 PM  
**After:** Monday 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM

### Scenario 2: Add Buffer Time
**Before:** Tuesday 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM, no buffers  
**Edit:** Add 15 min buffer before, 10 min after  
**After:** Tuesday 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM (15m before, 10m after)

### Scenario 3: Move to Different Day
**Before:** Wednesday 1:00 PM - 5:00 PM  
**Edit:** Change day to Thursday  
**After:** Thursday 1:00 PM - 5:00 PM
